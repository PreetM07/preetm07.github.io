This is a sample post. Write in **Markdown**: headings, lists, links and images all work.

## What to check

- Dates, numbers and currency in each region.
- Text that is cut off or overlaps after translation.
- Devanagari, Gujarati and Bengali characters rendering correctly.
- Placeholders such as `{name}` surviving translation.
